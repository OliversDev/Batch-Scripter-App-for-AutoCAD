Batch Scripter Privacy Policy

Publisher: Oliver Wackenreuther
Effective date: September 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to Batch Scripter, an application published by Oliver Wackenreuther through the Autodesk Design and Make Marketplace.

1. Data processed or collected by the application

Batch Scripter does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal information, drawing information, script content, usage information, or analytics.

The application locally processes paths to user-selected DWG and DWT files, AutoCAD script content entered by the user or read from a selected SCR or TXT file, and the user's choice to save or discard drawing changes. This information is used solely to perform the requested batch operation and is not transmitted to the Publisher or any third party.

Batch Scripter does not require a Publisher account and does not use telemetry, advertising, tracking technologies, or cloud-based processing.

2. Local temporary files and diagnostic logs

Batch Scripter may create temporary AutoCAD script files under %TEMP%\BatchScripter and diagnostic error logs under %LOCALAPPDATA%\Batch Scripter\Logs when an application error occurs.

Temporary scripts older than seven days are removed when Batch Scripter is subsequently opened. Diagnostic logs remain on the user's computer until the user deletes them. These local files are not uploaded or transmitted to the Publisher.

3. Third parties

Batch Scripter does not share application data with third parties. There are therefore no analytics providers, advertising networks, third-party SDK providers, affiliates, or other third parties receiving data from the application.

The application contains optional links to external websites, including GitHub and LinkedIn. A website is opened only when the user selects its link. Batch Scripter does not send drawing paths, drawing contents, script content, temporary scripts, or diagnostic logs to those websites. Use of an external website is governed by that website's own privacy policy.

Batch Scripter is distributed through the Autodesk Design and Make Marketplace. Information independently collected or processed by Autodesk in connection with Marketplace access, downloads, accounts, or transactions is governed by Autodesk's privacy policies and is not received through Batch Scripter.

If the Publisher ever introduces a third party that receives application data, this policy will be updated before that functionality is released, and the third party will be required to protect the data consistently with this policy and applicable law.

4. Support communications

If a user voluntarily contacts the Publisher for support, the Publisher may receive information the user chooses to provide, such as the user's name, email address, product version, problem description, screenshots, logs, drawings, or scripts. It is collected directly from the support communication and used only to respond, diagnose the reported issue, and improve the applicable product.

Users should remove confidential or unnecessary information before providing support materials. Support information is not sold, rented, used for advertising, or shared except when required by law or when a service provider is necessary to deliver the support communication. Any such service provider will be expected to protect personal information consistently with this policy and applicable privacy requirements.

5. Retention and deletion

Application data and locally created files remain under the user's control and follow the local retention periods described above. The Publisher does not possess these files and therefore cannot remotely delete them.

Information voluntarily provided for support may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to resolve the request, maintain an appropriate support record, comply with legal obligations, or address disputes. Information that is no longer reasonably required will be deleted or anonymized.

Users can delete local temporary scripts and diagnostic logs at any time using normal Windows file-management tools. Uninstalling Batch Scripter does not necessarily remove files already created in the user's Windows temporary or local application-data folders.

6. Consent withdrawal and deletion requests

Batch Scripter does not request consent for telemetry, advertising, tracking, or remote application-data collection because those activities are not performed. There is no remotely stored application data for a user to revoke consent for or ask the Publisher to delete.

Providing personal information in a support request is voluntary. A user may withdraw consent for its continued use or request deletion of personal information previously provided directly to the Publisher by using the support contact identified on Batch Scripter's Autodesk Design and Make Marketplace listing. Reasonable steps will be taken to verify and complete the request unless retention is required by law or for another legitimate purpose.

7. Security

Reasonable administrative and technical measures are used to protect personal information voluntarily submitted for support. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's privacy

Batch Scripter is a professional productivity tool and is not directed toward children. The Publisher does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to Batch Scripter, legal requirements, or privacy practices. The effective date at the top of the policy will be revised when changes are made. If Batch Scripter begins collecting or transmitting application data, this policy and the privacy notice included with the application will be updated before that functionality is released.

10. Contact

Privacy questions, consent withdrawals, and deletion requests may be submitted using the Publisher support contact shown on Batch Scripter's Autodesk Design and Make Marketplace listing.
